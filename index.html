    Two vectors are parallel when one is a scalar multiple of the other:

    \[\class{cu}{\mathbf{u}} \parallel \class{cd}{\mathbf{d}} \iff \class{cu}{\mathbf{u}} = k\,\class{cd}{\mathbf{d}} \text{ for one number } k\]

    The same \(k\) has to scale the \(\mathbf{i}\)-component and the \(\mathbf{j}\)-component. Change \(\class{cu}{\mathbf{u}}\) below or drag its grey tip on the graph and watch whether one \(k\) can do both jobs.
